---
title: "Airdrop a moment"
openapi: POST /moment/airdrop
---

Passing an **email** recipient resolves that inbox to a Privy wallet and airdrops onchain. It is not a request-path email send.

After the transfer is indexed, recipients with a **linked email** get a Resend notification (`In Process notification: you received an airdrop`). Telegram airdrop notify stays separate. Self-airdrops skip email. Provider (Gmail or otherwise) does not matter.
