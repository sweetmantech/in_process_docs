title: "Moments & collections"
description: "How moments, collections, metadata and sales fit together."
---

A **collection** is an ERC-1155 contract on Base. A **moment** is one token in it. People collect copies of a moment.

## Find a moment

A moment is identified by its collection address, token ID and chain ID. Every create response returns all three:

```json
{
  "contractAddress": "0x...",
  "tokenId": "1",
  "chainId": 8453
}
```

Pass them to [Get a moment](/api-reference/moment/get) and the other moment endpoints.

## Metadata

Each moment points to a metadata JSON on Arweave (`tokenMetadataURI`). It holds the name, description, cover `image` and the `content` file. See [Quickstart](/quickstart) for the format.

## Collections

The artist who creates a collection is its admin and can create moments in it. Admins can be [added](/api-reference/moment/add-permission) or [removed](/api-reference/moment/remove-permission).

Each artist also has a **default collection**, used when they post from the app or Telegram. Until they [set one](/api-reference/collections/set-default), it is their own collection named "Process", created the first time it is needed.

## Sales

Each moment has a [sale config](/api-reference/objects/sales-config): price, currency (ETH or USDC) and sale window. A price of `0` makes it free to collect. Change it later with [Set a sale](/api-reference/sale).

Revenue goes to the moment's [splits](/api-reference/objects/splits) if it has them. See [Wallets](/concepts/wallets) for who pays and who receives.
